About us

A small team, with software people actually use

TellezSoftware started from an everyday problem: businesses that keep their products in notebooks, spreadsheets and scattered WhatsApp photos. That information gets lost, duplicated and is never at hand when a customer asks.

We are an independent software project based in Nicaragua. We build simple tools, in Spanish, that run in the browser and adapt to the way a Nicaraguan business actually sells.
